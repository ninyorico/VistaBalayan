Create a modern and professional web-based tourism management system UI/UX design in Figma for a capstone project titled:

“VistaBalayan: A Web-Based Tourism Data Analytics and Decision Support System for Visitor Monitoring in Balayan, Batangas”

Design Style:

* Clean, modern, minimal, and professional government-style dashboard
* White background with grayscale, black, and dark gray color palette
* Use subtle blue accents for buttons, charts, and highlights
* Rounded cards with soft shadows
* Responsive desktop-first layout
* Consistent spacing and typography
* Use modern sans-serif fonts like Inter or Poppins
* Organized and easy-to-navigate interface
* Focus on readability and data visualization
* Tourism and analytics inspired aesthetic
* Avoid overly colorful or playful designs

System Roles:

1. Municipal Tourism Officer
2. Establishment Staff

Role Description:

* Municipal Tourism Officer manages tourism data, monitors submissions, reviews reports, analyzes tourism trends, and accesses analytics dashboards and AI insights.
* Establishment Staff are responsible for encoding and submitting visitor and accommodation reports to the system.
